How do I inquire about and confirm my transfer?
Choose the route, date and number of passengers. Fill in your details and prepare your inquiry. When WhatsApp is enabled, you can open the chat with a ready-to-send message. The booking is confirmed only once we agree on the fare and availability with you.
How much does a transfer cost?
The fare depends on the route, the number of passengers and whether you need a round trip. That’s why we quote each journey: send us the details and we will agree on the price before confirming.
Can I arrange both the outbound and return trips?
Yes, you can request both in the same inquiry. Select “Round trip” and tell us your approximate times. We coordinate meeting points and availability before confirming.
What if I am traveling with children or a lot of luggage?
Tell us how many adults and children are traveling, their ages and the approximate amount of luggage. If you need a child restraint system, ask about its availability before booking. Each passenger must have a seat and the luggage must be compatible with the vehicle.
Are tickets to the waterfalls included?
No. The service is a transfer. Tickets to Iguazú National Park, activities and guided visits are purchased separately. Our waterfall route corresponds to the Argentine side.
